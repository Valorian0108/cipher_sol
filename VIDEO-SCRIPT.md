# Video script skeleton (record Sunday morning, before 10 AM)

This is a skeleton, not lines to read. Every scene says what is on screen
and the point to make. Say the point in your own everyday words, the card
story you already tell. Your rule: nothing gets said that you cannot
explain. Target length: about 3 minutes.

## Before you press record (10 minutes)

1. In krypt-solana/agent: run `npm run demo` and `npm run vendor`.
2. Open http://localhost:4200. Check the SETUP screen: five rows green.
3. Press TOP UP BUDGET once, so the twenty-call errand has room.
4. Close every other tab and app notification. Phone on silent.
5. Do one full rehearsal without recording. The real take is always
   smoother after one dry run.

## Scene 1: The problem (15s, screen: HOME)

On screen: the big title and the three step boxes.
The point: you own a business and you have a worker you trust to work, but
not to carry your money. In the old days you hand him cash and hope. You
cannot watch his every step.

## Scene 2: The one-sentence answer (10s, screen: HOME)

The point: so instead of cash, you give him a prepaid card with your
conditions built in, enforced like a bank enforces them. That card is a
program on Solana, and this page is the market where he uses it.

## Scene 3: Opening the card (30s, screen: SETUP)

On screen: the five rows, all green, each with an ON CHAIN pill.
The point, row by row in plain words: whose card it is, the four
conditions (nothing over the limit, big buys ask me, daily total, approved
shops only), the worker named on it, the shops approved, the money loaded.
Click one explorer link to show it is a real public record, not a drawing.

## Scene 4: The machine shops alone (45s, screen: SHOP, task 1)

On screen: click BUILD THE SOL MARKET REPORT. Rows stream: green pills,
deliveries.
The point: twenty small purchases, each under your ask-me line, so the
card pays them alone. Nobody is watching and nothing can go wrong, because
the card checks your conditions on every single swipe. This is the money
shot of the whole product. Let it stream while you talk.

## Scene 5: The big purchase waits for you (40s, screen: SHOP, task 2)

On screen: run COMMISSION THE DEEP RESEARCH BRIEF. Five small ones settle,
then the amber card appears. Read it aloud, then click APPROVE.
The point: this one is above your line, so the card stops and asks you.
The worker cannot answer for you. Only the owner key can say yes, and the
worker does not hold it. After the yes, the money moves and the goods are
handed over.

## Scene 6: The card says no (30s, screen: SHOP, task 3)

On screen: run BUY FROM THE CHEAPEST BIDDER. Two red declines with reasons.
The point: a shop you never approved, and an item over your limit. Both
declined, with the reason written on the receipt, and not one cent moved.
The declines are the feature. A gate that cannot say no is not a gate.

## Scene 7: The worker comes home (20s, screen: REPORT)

On screen: the report summary and the ledger table.
The point: the job you asked for, built from what was actually delivered,
with every receipt stapled under it. Click one proof link.

## Scene 8: The receipt book (25s, screen: AUDIT)

On screen: your conditions on the record, and the full decision table.
The point: every swipe the card ever made, including declines, in order,
each one a public record. Nobody has to take my word for anything. This
is what a shopkeeper sees too, and it is why a shop can trust a machine
buyer: no goods leave without a settled receipt naming them.

## Scene 9: Close (20s, any screen or camera on you)

The point, three sentences: what it is (a spending gate for AI agents on
Solana, program address on screen or in the description), what is
verified (a five-attack suite where every prediction held, all on public
devnet), and what is next (a consumer version where a stranger signs up
with a passkey, sees dollars, never sees a key: already being built in a
second app).

## If something goes wrong mid-take

- Slow transaction: keep talking, the feed catches up. Do not restart.
- A task errors: press TOP UP BUDGET on SETUP and run it again.
- Worst case: record scenes separately. Nobody requires one continuous
  take.
